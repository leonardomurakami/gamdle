# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who enjoy short daily games, probability puzzles, and comparing results with friends. They visit for a focused five-minute session and want meaningful risk decisions without real-money stakes.

## Product Purpose

Gamdle gives every player the same daily hidden casino landscape. Players allocate a shared fictional bankroll across twelve global moves, deciding which table to visit, how much to risk, and when to leave. Success is a complete, trustworthy daily run that is fun to share and compare.

## Brand Personality

Playful, kinetic, and candid. The casino feels like an electronic arcade betting floor with physical games and immediate feedback, never a real-money gambling service.

## Anti-references

Avoid realistic betting apps, crypto casinos, dark luxury casino styling, manipulative retention mechanics, purchasable chips, fake urgency, and interfaces that conceal odds.

## Design Principles

- Make the next decision obvious while keeping its outcome suspenseful.
- Show odds and possible returns before every commitment.
- Let the wheel, cards, dice, and reels visibly perform every resolved outcome.
- Celebrate unlikely stories without implying skill where luck decided the result.
- Treat stopping early as a legitimate decision.
- Keep money, purchases, and redeemable value entirely outside the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Never rely on color alone for outcomes, preserve strong focus states, support keyboard play and reduced motion, and use plain language for probability and payout information.
